# HSK 1 Mandarin Prep Dashboard

A free browser-based dashboard for studying HSK 1 Mandarin vocabulary, pinyin, tones, listening, reading, and sentence structure.

This project was created as a labor of love for beginner Mandarin learners who want a structured, interactive way to prepare for HSK 1.

## Features

- 38-day HSK 1 study plan
- 150 HSK 1 vocabulary words
- 4 new words per day
- Chinese characters, pinyin, English, and French translations
- Vocabulary grouped by verbs, pronouns, nouns, adjectives, and grammar words
- Day and week completion tracking
- Local progress saving in the browser
- Stroke order links for each Chinese character
- Flashcards:
  - Character → Pinyin
  - Pinyin → English
  - English → Pinyin
  - Word listening
  - Mistake review
- Tone drill practice
- Full-sentence listening quiz
- Sentence builder for Mandarin word order
- HSK 1 grammar notes
- HSK 1 exam information and official resource links
- Export data option for backing up progress

## How It Works

The dashboard is designed around a 38-day plan.

Each day introduces 4 vocabulary words. When a day is marked complete, those words become available in the review tools, flashcards, tone drills, listening practice, and sentence builder.

This helps the dashboard grow with the learner instead of showing all practice material at once.

## Local Progress

Progress is saved locally in your browser using `localStorage`.

This means:

- No account is required.
- Your progress stays on your device.
- Your data is not uploaded to a server.
- If you clear browser data, switch browsers, or use another device, your progress will not automatically follow you.

Use the **Export data** option to copy a backup of your progress, notes, custom vocabulary, mistakes, and practice stats.

## HSK Version Note

This dashboard is built for the HSK 1 vocabulary structure commonly associated with HSK 2.0, using the 150-word HSK 1 list.

Always check the official Chinese Test website for the most current exam requirements before registering for an exam.
